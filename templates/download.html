Platform Features & Guidelines

Ultimate Control for Teachers

  • Versatile Question Creation: Create highly customized exams supporting multiple question types. Assign specific marks for each question individually.
  • MCQ Automated Grading MCQ : Set up 4-option multiple-choice questions. The system automatically grades them and calculates negative marking instantly.
  • Subjective Smart Evaluation Descriptive : Ask long/descriptive questions. Students will upload images of their handwritten answers. You can view these images directly in your dashboard, zoom in, and manually award marks which instantly reflect in their final score.
  • AI Smart Paste (Gemini AI): Don't type manually! Upload your PDF/Image to Gemini or ChatGPT, extract the questions, and use our "Smart Paste" to load all questions instantly.
  • Privacy & Security: Create "Public" exams visible to all, or "Private" exams locked behind a secret Exam Code that only your specific students can access.
  • Advanced Analytics: Monitor class averages, individual student attempts, and specific answer sheets from a dedicated analytics dashboard.

Seamless Experience for Students

  • Dynamic Exam Interface: Answer MCQs with a single tap, and utilize our built-in Smart Camera to capture, crop, and upload handwritten answers for subjective questions without leaving the exam page.
  • Live Feed & Recommendations: Discover exams tailored to your class and subject. Search for specific teachers or exam codes effortlessly.
  • Instant Results & Detailed Review: Get your MCQ scores immediately. Once teachers evaluate your uploaded subjective papers, your updated total score and detailed review will be visible.
  • Progress Tracking: Level up your profile! Your avatar changes color dynamically based on your overall performance and consistency.

Military-Grade Anti-Cheat System

  • Tab/App Switching Detection: If a student tries to minimize the app, switch tabs, or open Google Lens, the system immediately registers a warning.
  • Auto-Submission Protocol: The system allows a maximum of 5 warnings. On the 5th violation, the exam is automatically terminated and submitted.
  • Smart Pausing: During the subjective image upload process, the anti-cheat system intelligently pauses itself to allow students to use their camera, resuming instantly upon return.
  • Copy & Screenshot Block: Text copying is strictly disabled, and taking screenshots within the exam room is entirely blocked (on supported Android versions).
